Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Flowerday is a period calendar application for iPhone, iPad and Android. This page explains what happens with your information when you use it.

In short: Flowerday doesn't collect any data. What you enter stays on your device, and in your device's own backups if you allow them.

What you enter

The days you mark (period days and, if you turn them on, sex and medication) and your settings are saved in the app on your device. The Home Screen widget reads them on the same device. Nothing is sent to the app's developer or to anyone else.

What the app collects

Nothing. Flowerday has no account or sign-up, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. On Android the app doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

Backups

So that you don't lose your records when you lose or change your phone, Flowerday lets your device's own backup keep them. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Data → "Back up my records". Then your records stay only on this device.

  • Android: your records go into your phone's Google backup, if backup is turned on in your phone's settings. On Android 9 and later this backup is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock.
  • iPhone and iPad: your records go into your iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer, if you use them.

These backups belong to you and are managed by Google, Apple and your own settings. The app's developer has no access to them.

Deleting your data

Settings → Data → "Erase all records" deletes every marked day. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Backups that already exist are managed in your Google or iCloud settings.

Health information

Period and fertility information is sensitive. Flowerday never sends it anywhere, so it is never seen, sold or shared by anyone behind the app. Flowerday is not a medical device, and its predictions are estimates.

Children

Flowerday collects no data from anyone, including children.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security (see GitHub's Privacy Statement). No analytics or cookies are added.
