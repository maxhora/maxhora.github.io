How do I mark my period?

Tap a day in the calendar. Tap it again to remove the mark.

How do I add the widget?

iPhone and iPad: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Flowerday. Android: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Widgets, and choose Flowerday. On Android you can resize it.

Why did my next period date change?

Predictions are estimates based on your own history. They get better as you mark more cycles.

Will my records move to a new phone?

Yes, if "Back up my records" is on (it is by default) and your phone's backup is on. Restoring your Google, iCloud or computer backup on the new phone brings them back. Flowerday has no account or cloud sync of its own.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language.

Is Flowerday a medical app?

No. Its predictions are estimates. Don't use it for birth control or to plan a pregnancy, and talk to a doctor about health questions.
